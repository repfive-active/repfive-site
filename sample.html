10-Day Soccer Challenge

Watch. Notice. Try it yourself.

Day 1
Scan Before You Receive
⚽ Sport
Before the ball gets to you, find out what's around you so you can make your next decision earlier.
Day 2
One Word
🤝 Team
Communication changes a teammate's action; a quick word can give them information that supports them to make their next move.
Day 3
Pass and Move
⚽ Sport
Passing is not the end of your involvement; move to create the next option.
Day 4
Call for the Ball
🤝 Team
How you communicate can make it easier for your teammate to trust you and pass the ball.
Day 5
Defend the 1v1
⚽ Sport
Good 1v1 defending means slowing the attacker down, staying in control, and waiting for the right moment.
Day 6
Pro Eyes
🎮 Fun
Look for clues, predict what might happen next by noticing the players, space and possibilities around the ball.
Day 7
Keep It Simple
🌟 Life
When things feel complicated, focusing on the simple things you can control can help you make better decisions.
Day 8
Next Chance
🌟 Life
Even the best players make mistakes. What matters is how you respond when the next opportunity comes.
Day 9
Soft First Touch
⚽ Sport
A good first touch keeps the ball close and gives you more control over what you do next.
Day 10
The First 3 Seconds
⚽ Sport
When the team loses the ball, the first few seconds matter. Know your job and help the team defend.
10 days. 10 reps. Watch it. Notice it. Try it.
