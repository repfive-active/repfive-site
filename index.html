Who RepFive Is For

Built for the next stage of the athlete journey.

RepFive is intentionally focused on middle-school and high-school athletes—when structured development and team identity become increasingly important.

Elementary School

Explore. Play. Move.

RepFive isn't designed to replace the freedom, exploration, outdoor play, and unstructured activity that young children need.

RepFive Sweet Spot

Middle School → High School

Athletes are ready for more intentional learning, tactical thinking, skill development, and team goals—without losing the fun of being part of a team.

How It Works

Simple enough to do every day.

No complicated training plans. No coach verification. No screen-time surveillance. Just a small daily Rep that moves the team forward.

01

Watch

Watch a short, coach-curated lesson focused on a specific skill or game concept.

02

Think

Answer a couple of multiple-choice questions to reinforce what you just watched.

03

Earn

A parent confirms that the athlete completed the day's Rep and the athlete earns 20 points.

04

Move the Team

Individual Reps add up to a shared team goal and a real-world reward.
